# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept technical feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Be honest about what you tested and what you didn't.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or attempts to inflate stars, installs, or other metrics.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider harmful.

To report a problem, use [GitHub's private vulnerability and abuse reporting](https://github.com/Piyushverma122/React-Architecture-Skill/security) or contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Piyushverma122). Reports are handled privately.
